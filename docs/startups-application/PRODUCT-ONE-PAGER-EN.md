# Istanbul Research Assistant

**Source-linked research across Istanbul's 39 districts.**

**Founder:** Şaban Durali, individual founder; no company has been incorporated for this product. Personal brand: real estate, consulting, research and technology.

**Problem and audience.** District research draws on scattered publications with different dates and scopes. The prototype serves people exploring Istanbul, prospective residents, local researchers and professionals doing initial desk research. These are intended audiences, not a claimed customer base.

**Working prototype.** Ask a question in Turkish, choose one or two districts, and inspect original editorial excerpts with source links and dates. The corpus contains 39 published guides and 391 source records. Comparisons show relevant passages alongside each other; unsupported questions receive an explicit limitation message. The English page introduces the product; the research interaction is Turkish.

**Evidence, not traction.** The 402 previously verified Production records become 391 demo records under an existing publication filter: eight lack required data dates and three fall outside the official-domain allowlist. Records are not unique pages. No customer, revenue, funding or adoption metrics are claimed.

**Claude integration.** A server-side adapter is prepared for a controlled local pilot. Claude would select IDs from at most six candidate public excerpts; the application validates the selection and renders original text. Live model validation is incomplete. The protected hosted demo uses free keyword-based selection and never calls Claude.

**Reliability and safeguards.** Sources are associated with sections, not independently verified per sentence. Editorial dates are not a freshness guarantee. No live prices, investment promises or building safety assessments. The demo has no private CMS/database access; the application does not save queries. The future pilot sends the question and bounded public evidence to Anthropic. Explicit approval, request/token limits and a process-local budget safeguard apply; they do not guarantee a total account billing cap.

**Next milestone.** Complete an approved, supervised model evaluation, then plan 3–5 volunteer research sessions to test source inspection and comprehension of limitations. No live pilot or user study has been completed. Program support would help evaluate this workflow and obtain technical feedback; benefits are not guaranteed.
